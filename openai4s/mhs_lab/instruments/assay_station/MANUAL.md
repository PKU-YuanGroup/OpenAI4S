# AS-96 enzyme-inhibition assay workstation (simulated)

This is the specification sheet of a simulated instrument. No physical AS-96 exists.

## Operating limits

- Plate: one 96-well plate, rows A-H, columns 1-12, 100 uL working volume per well.
- Dose series: 3 to 10 points in columns 1-10 of one row; column 11 is the vehicle control, column 12 the no-enzyme blank.
- Top concentration in the well: 0.001 to 200 uM (DMSO stays at or below 1 %).
- Serial dilution factor: 1.5 to 10.
- Substrate concentration: 5 to 500 uM.
- Incubation temperature: 20 to 40 Cel.
- Kinetic read: 405 or 410 nm; duration 60 to 1800 s; interval 10 to 120 s.
- Compound stocks are 10 mM in DMSO unless a LIMS vial label states otherwise.

## Safety

- The emergency stop halts the liquid handler and the reader; nothing is dispensed after it latches.
- The reader requires the lid closed.
