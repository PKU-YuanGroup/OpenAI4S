# FR-10 continuous-flow reactor with inline HPLC (simulated)

This is the specification sheet of a simulated instrument. No physical FR-10 exists.

## Operating limits

- Reactor: 10 mL PFA coil, two HPLC pumps (substrate A, amine B), back-pressure regulator at 8 bar.
- Coil temperature: 25 to 150 Cel.
- Residence time: 0.5 to 20 min (pump flow 0.5 to 20 mL/min).
- Amine equivalents (B per A): 1.0 to 2.0.
- Substrate A concentration in the coil: 0.1 to 1.0 mol/L.
- Loaded amines: B1 aniline, B2 4-fluoroaniline, B3 4-methoxyaniline.
- Product collection: 1 to 50 mL per vial, labelled in the LIMS.
- Inline HPLC samples the outlet after three reactor volumes (steady state).

## Safety

- The emergency stop switches both pumps and the heater off and vents to the BPR.
- Maximum system pressure 20 bar.
